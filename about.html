About

We built the app we wished we'd had.

hiro exists because the path from a first university course to an H-1B is the hardest part of being an international student, and no one was building for the years after the offer.

The moment you hear the word "hired" as an international student, a clock you didn't know existed starts ticking. OPT gives you a runway measured in days, the H-1B is a lottery you can't study for, and you're supposed to figure all of it out while doing the actual job. I went through it the way most of us do, alone, with a school dashboard that only ever helped me land the offer and then went quiet the second I needed it most. I built hiro so the next student doesn't have to wing the hardest years of their life, and instead has a co-pilot from their first course all the way to H-1B.

- Axel, founder
