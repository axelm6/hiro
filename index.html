04 · Why now

Three forces, one window.

The pieces that make Hiro possible only lined up recently. Building it a year ago would have been too early. Waiting a year is too late.

AI
Matching finally works

Resume parsing, match scoring, and listing drafts that used to need a recruiter now run in seconds. The swipe is only useful because the ranking behind it is good.

Visa
The stakes have never been higher

OPT timelines and H-1B odds drive every decision an international student makes. No incumbent tracks them. We make the deadline impossible to miss.

Legacy
The incumbents are dashboards

The incumbents show the work; they don't do it. Universities have paid for inadequate before, which makes the wedge a replacement, not a new line item.
